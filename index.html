Contour Dots

Depth field → flow field → streamlines → oriented dots.

Depth, not brightness. White reads as near, black as far. Threshold carves the black negative space; nothing is drawn there.

Luminance is only a proxy for depth. Estimate depth runs Depth Anything V2 in the browser and feeds the pipeline real geometry instead — served over http, and one download on first use.

Rotation of every dot is atan2(flow.y, flow.x), and flow is the depth gradient turned ninety degrees: F = (-∂D/∂y, ∂D/∂x).
